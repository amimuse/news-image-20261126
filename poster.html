WORLD NEWS

Seven stories that defined the day — trade, conflict, disaster and a Thanksgiving first in 100 years.
14PARTNERS
TRADE  •  WASHINGTON

US proposes new tariffs on 14 trading partners

Washington proposed duties of 18–20% on several Asian exporters, citing excess industrial capacity. China, the EU and Japan are among the 14 named. Beijing called it political manipulation; the EU and Japan rejected the case.

MIDDLE EAST • KUWAIT
2 KILLED

Drone attack in Kuwait kills two Italians

Two Rheinmetall Italia technicians died at an air-defence site; four others were hurt. Kuwait blamed Iran. Tehran denied any role.

DISASTER • CAIRO
31 KILLED

Cairo tanker fire kills 31

A fuel tanker overturned on Cairo’s Ring Road and burst into flames. 21 people were injured. The driver was detained after police said he was speeding.

EUROPE • FRANKFURT
DEC DECISION

New chief picked to lead the ECB

Spain’s Pablo Hernández de Cos, now head of the Bank for International Settlements, will succeed Christine Lagarde. Formal appointment is expected in December.

ASIA • PYONGYANG
NUCLEAR

Kim vows a bigger nuclear arsenal

Kim Jong Un visited nuclear facilities and called for wider use of tactical nuclear weapons, saying North Korea’s nuclear status “will never change”.

DISASTER • BANGKOK
9 INJURED

Blast at Bangkok oil facility injures nine

An explosion at a Bangchak refinery sent smoke over the city. Three people were critically hurt and nearby residents were urged to leave.

USA • NEW YORK
100 YEARS

Macy’s parade postponed — a first in 100 years

A storm forced the Thanksgiving Day Parade to Saturday, 28 Nov. About 300,000 lost power and hundreds of flights were cancelled.
